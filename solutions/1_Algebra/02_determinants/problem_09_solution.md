# Exercise 9

Place the two vectors as columns:
$$
M=\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&4\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Then
$$
\det M=3\cdot4-1\cdot1=11.
$$

The area of the parallelogram is the absolute value of this determinant:
$$
\mathcal A=|\det M|=11.
$$

Reversing the order of the vectors gives
$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\4&1\end{pmatrix}
=1-12=-11.
$$

Thus interchanging the vectors reverses the sign of the determinant, but the geometric area remains
$$
|-11|=11.
$$
