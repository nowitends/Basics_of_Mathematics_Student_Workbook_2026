# Exercise 4

The matrix is upper triangular, so its determinant equals the product of the diagonal entries:
$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}
2&4&1\\
0&-3&5\\
0&0&7
\end{pmatrix}
=2\cdot(-3)\cdot7=-42.
$$

In general, for any triangular square matrix,
$$
\det A=\prod_i a_{ii}.
$$
The entries above or below the diagonal do not affect this product.
