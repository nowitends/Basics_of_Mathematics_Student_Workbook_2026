# Exercise 8

The determinant is multiplicative, so
$$
\det(AB)=\det A\,\det B=3(-5)=-15.
$$
Similarly,
$$
\det(BA)=\det B\,\det A=(-5)3=-15.
$$

Because $\det A=3\neq0$, the inverse $A^{-1}$ exists and
$$
\det(A^{-1})=\frac{1}{\det A}=\frac13.
$$
Hence
$$
\det(A^{-1}B)
=\det(A^{-1})\det B
=\frac13(-5)
=-\frac53.
$$

The equality $\det(AB)=\det(BA)$ follows from multiplication of the scalar determinants. It does not imply $AB=BA$.
