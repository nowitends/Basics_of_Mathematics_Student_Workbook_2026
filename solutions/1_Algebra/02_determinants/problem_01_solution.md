# Exercise 1

For a $2\times2$ matrix,
$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}=ad-bc.
$$

Therefore,
$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}3&-2\\5&4\end{pmatrix}
=3\cdot4-(-2)\cdot5
=12+10=22,
$$
while
$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}1&7\\2&14\end{pmatrix}
=1\cdot14-7\cdot2
=0.
$$

A square matrix is invertible exactly when its determinant is nonzero. Hence the first matrix is invertible and the second one is not.
