# Exercise 7

Since $A$ is $3\times3$ and $\det A=-4$,
$$
\det(2A)=2^3\det A=8(-4)=-32.
$$

Transposition does not change the determinant:
$$
\det(A^T)=\det A=-4.
$$

Using multiplicativity,
$$
\det(A^2)=\det(A)\det(A)=(-4)^2=16.
$$

Finally,
$$
\det(-A)=(-1)^3\det A=-1\cdot(-4)=4.
$$

Thus the four determinants are
$$
-32,\qquad -4,\qquad 16,\qquad 4.
$$
