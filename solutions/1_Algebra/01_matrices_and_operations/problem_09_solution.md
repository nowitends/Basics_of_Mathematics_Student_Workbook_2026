# Exercise 9

First scaling:
$$Sx=\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\end{pmatrix}$$
and then rotation:
$$RSx=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\2\end{pmatrix}.$$

In the other order
$$Rx=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
SRx=\begin{pmatrix}-4\\1\end{pmatrix}.$$

They are different because scaling only changes the horizontal direction, so doing it before or after rotation changes which direction is being stretched.
