$$\begin{pmatrix}1&2&-1\\2&4&1\\-1&1&3\end{pmatrix}.$$

$$\begin{pmatrix}1&2&-1\\0&0&3\\-1&1&3\end{pmatrix}$$

$$\begin{pmatrix}1&2&-1\\0&0&3\\0&3&2\end{pmatrix}$$

$$\begin{pmatrix}1&2&-1\\0&3&2\\0&0&3\end{pmatrix}.$$
