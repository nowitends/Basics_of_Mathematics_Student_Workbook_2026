# Exercise 10

$$b_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\\3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
b_2=\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}.$$

$$Ab_1=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Ab_2=\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix}.$$

Also
$$AB=\begin{pmatrix}-1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

So column 1 of $AB$ is $Ab_1$ and column 2 is $Ab_2$. This comes directly from how matrix multiplication is defined.
