# Exercise 7

For the identity and zero matrix:
$$AI=IA=A,\qquad A+0=A.$$

The powers are
$$A^2=\begin{pmatrix}4&4\\0&4\end{pmatrix},$$
$$A^3=\begin{pmatrix}8&12\\0&8\end{pmatrix}.$$

The diagonal is powers of 2. The upper right entry also follows a pattern. In fact it looks like
$$A^n=\begin{pmatrix}2^n&n2^{n-1}\\0&2^n\end{pmatrix}.$$
